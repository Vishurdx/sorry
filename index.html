Page 1 of 4 • A Letter from My Heart 🌻
🌻 From Vishu

Hi My love of life,

I honestly don't even know how to start this, but I just want to say I'm really sorry.

I know I've not been the boyfriend you needed me to be over the past few months. I've been so caught up with office work, responsibilities and everything happening around me that I somehow forgot that you also need my time, attention and presence. And the worst part is that there were days when I didn't just get busy, I actually ignored you. I know how that must have made you feel, and it hurts me to realise that I've been the reason behind it.

I know you never asked for anything extraordinary. You just wanted a little of my time, a few conversations, some attention, and to feel that you're important to me. And I'm sorry that I made even those little things feel like too much to ask for.

"You are my sunflower 🌻 — the brightest, warmest part of every single day. I never want you to feel that you have to beg for my time or that you have to keep reminding me that you exist in my life. You shouldn't have to do that. You deserve to feel loved without having to ask for it."

I know saying that I was busy with work doesn't justify making you feel ignored or unimportant. You have your own feelings, your own expectations, and you deserve to be heard too. Maybe I've been so occupied with managing everything else that I haven't realised how much I've been neglecting us.

I miss the way things used to be. I miss talking to you without constantly looking at the clock or thinking about work. I miss giving you my undivided attention. And honestly, I don't want us to become two people who are together but barely have time for each other.

I'm not saying all this just because I don't want you to be upset with me. I'm saying it because I genuinely don't want to lose the little things that make us us.

I can't promise that I'll never be busy, but I can promise that I don't want to make you feel alone in this relationship again. I want to make an effort, be more present and show you through my actions that you matter to me.

I'm sorry for all the times I made you wait, all the times I didn't listen, and all the times you needed me but I wasn't there.

I love you. And I really hope I can make you feel that, not just through my words but through the way I treat you every single day. ❤️

🌻
Forever Yours, Vishu 💜

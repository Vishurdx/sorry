## 🧸 2. Adding WhatsApp Stickers (Page 3)

### Folder Location:
📁 `d:\Baby\assets\stickers\`

### What to name your stickers:
Copy your WhatsApp stickers (or cute meme / romantic sticker images) into `assets\stickers\` and name them:
- `sticker1.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker2.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker3.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker4.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker5.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker6.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker7.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)
- `sticker8.png` (or `.webp` / `.jpg`)

### 💡 How to get WhatsApp Stickers:
1. **From WhatsApp Web**: Right-click any sticker in your chat with her → Click **"Save image as..."** → Save it into `assets\stickers\` as `sticker1.png`, etc.
2. **From your phone**: Share or email the sticker image to yourself, or use any PNG/WebP sticker!
3. **From the Website directly**: You can click the **📷 change icon** on any sticker card on Page 3 to pick a sticker image directly from your files!
